1. Problem & Motivation

Problem Statement

Fragmented Information
Campus events are trapped in scattered private groups, making them invisible.
Blind Spontaneity
Students lack real-time spatial awareness of what is happening around them.

Why MeetHere?

Existing social tools fail to connect immediate intent with precise geographic location.

Frictionless Discovery
Map-first UI makes joining events as easy as walking to them.
AI-Driven Relevance
Integrated LLM agents match users' vibe with campus activities.

2. Target Users

Primary Audience

HKU Students (Undergraduates & Postgraduates)

The Spontaneous

Need: Instant social engagement during sudden academic breaks.

"Anyone want to grab coffee near Centennial Campus right now?"

The Newcomer

Need: Low-friction ways to build new social circles and integrate.

The Hobbyist

Need: Finding partners for niche offline activities on short notice.

3. Background Research

Meetup

Strength: Mature ecosystem for long-term interest-based communities.

Shortcoming: High cognitive load for planning; unsuitable for "right now" needs.

Improvement: Implement geofenced, low-commitment event creation flows.

Apple Invites

Strength: Seamless iOS integration with zero learning curve for Apple users.

Shortcoming: Private and invite-only; no public discovery layer.

Improvement: Build a campus-wide public board for community-based discovery.

WhatsApp

Strength: Ubiquitous communication tool with highest daily active user base.

Shortcoming: Text-only and fragmented; impossible to browse geographically.

Improvement: Consolidate fragmented chat data into a unified spatial map interface.
